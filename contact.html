Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our platform

How do I get started as a student freelancer?

Simply create your profile, showcase your skills and portfolio, set your rates, and start applying to relevant projects. Our verification process ensures you get access to quality opportunities.

What types of projects are available?

We offer projects across web development, mobile apps, graphic design, content writing, digital marketing, data analysis, and more. Projects range from short-term tasks to long-term collaborations.

How does payment work?

We use secure escrow payments. Clients fund projects upfront, and payments are released to freelancers upon milestone completion or project delivery. We support multiple payment methods.

Is there a fee for using the platform?

Creating a profile is free. We charge a small service fee (10%) on completed projects, which covers platform maintenance, payment processing, and support services.
